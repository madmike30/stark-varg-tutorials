# How to remove and install the Hand Guards on your Stark VARG EX

Source video: `How to remove and install the Hand Guards on your Stark VARG EX` by Stark Future Official

Applicable models: `EX`

## Scope

This guide follows the same step-by-step workshop structure as the MX tutorials, but it is derived as a visual sequence from the official Stark Future ASMR video. Because the EX video does not provide usable captions or narration, the procedure is documented through curated screenshots and concise action guidance.

## Preparation

- Place the bike on a stable stand or work area before starting the procedure.
- Prepare the correct tools for the fasteners, clips, brackets, and components shown in the video.
- Keep removed hardware organized in sequence so reassembly follows the same order as the video.
- Have a torque wrench ready for any tightening steps that specify a torque value.

## Removal Procedure

### 1. Remove the retaining hardware for the Hand Guards

Remove the visible bolts, screws, or clips that hold the Hand Guards in place.

Keep track of any washers, collars, or spacers as each fastener is removed.

![Step 1 - remove the retaining hardware for the hand guards](assets/screenshots/step_01_remove-the-retaining-hardware-for-the-hand-guards.jpg)

### 2. Release any support piece attached to the Hand Guards

Remove any bracket, spacer, clamp, or secondary fastener that still retains the Hand Guards.

Note the hardware stack order so the same arrangement is used during assembly.

![Step 2 - release any support piece attached to the hand guards](assets/screenshots/step_02_release-any-support-piece-attached-to-the-hand-guards.jpg)

### 3. Remove the Hand Guards from its mounting position

Slide or lift the Hand Guards free once the retaining hardware is removed.

Inspect the mounting surfaces and the removed hardware before installation begins.

![Step 3 - remove the hand guards from its mounting position](assets/screenshots/step_03_remove-the-hand-guards-from-its-mounting-position.jpg)

## Installation Procedure

### 4. Position the Hand Guards for installation

Place the Hand Guards back into its correct orientation on the bike.

Align the mounting holes and support points before any hardware is tightened.

![Step 4 - position the hand guards for installation](assets/screenshots/step_04_position-the-hand-guards-for-installation.jpg)

### 5. Reinstall any spacer, bracket, or clip for the Hand Guards

Return each spacer, support piece, or clip to the same position used during removal.

Confirm the hardware stack is seated flat and centered before the main fasteners are installed.

![Step 5 - reinstall any spacer, bracket, or clip for the hand guards](assets/screenshots/step_05_reinstall-any-spacer-bracket-or-clip-for-the-hand-guards.jpg)

### 6. Install the retaining hardware for the Hand Guards

Install the mounting fasteners for the Hand Guards and tighten them evenly while holding the part aligned.

Check that the part sits flush and does not interfere with nearby moving parts.

![Step 6 - install the retaining hardware for the hand guards](assets/screenshots/step_06_install-the-retaining-hardware-for-the-hand-guards.jpg)

### 7. Verify the clearance and alignment of the Hand Guards

Inspect the completed fitment of the Hand Guards and confirm the surrounding area has proper clearance.

Make sure no hardware, clip, or support piece is missing before the bike is returned to service.

![Step 7 - verify the clearance and alignment of the hand guards](assets/screenshots/step_07_verify-the-clearance-and-alignment-of-the-hand-guards.jpg)

## Critical Checks Before Riding

- Confirm the serviced component is fully seated and all fasteners are tightened in the correct order.
- Recheck all torque-critical hardware before riding.
- Make sure all routed cables, clips, brackets, and surrounding parts are returned to their original positions.
- Inspect the bike visually for any missing hardware before returning it to service.
