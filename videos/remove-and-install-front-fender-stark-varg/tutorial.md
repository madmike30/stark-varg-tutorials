# Remove and Install Front Fender - Stark VARG

Source video: `Remove and Install Front Fender - Stark VARG` by Stark Future Official

Applicable models: `MX`

## Scope

This guide follows the same step-by-step workshop structure as the MX tutorials, but it is derived as a visual sequence from the official Stark Future ASMR video. Because the source video does not provide usable captions or narration, the procedure is documented through curated screenshots and concise action guidance.

## Preparation

- Place the bike on a stable stand or work area before starting the procedure.
- Prepare the correct tools for the fasteners, clips, brackets, and components shown in the video.
- Keep removed hardware organized in sequence so reassembly follows the same order as the video.
- Have a torque wrench ready for any tightening steps that specify a torque value.

## Removal Procedure

### 1. Prepare access to the Front Fender

Remove the surrounding parts needed to expose the Front Fender mounting points shown in the video.

Keep the removed fasteners organized in sequence so reassembly matches the video.

![Step 1 - prepare access to the front fender](assets/screenshots/step_01_prepare-access-to-the-front-fender.jpg)

### 2. Remove the visible retaining hardware for the Front Fender

Remove the visible fasteners that secure the Front Fender and keep the hardware in removal order.

Support the component as the last fastener is removed.

![Step 2 - remove the visible retaining hardware for the front fender](assets/screenshots/step_02_remove-the-visible-retaining-hardware-for-the-front-fender.jpg)

### 3. Release any bracket, clip, or coupling attached to the Front Fender

Free any bracket, clip, spacer, linkage, or connector that still ties the Front Fender to the bike.

Note the orientation of these supporting pieces before setting them aside.

![Step 3 - release any bracket, clip, or coupling attached to the front fender](assets/screenshots/step_03_release-any-bracket-clip-or-coupling-attached-to-the-front-f.jpg)

### 4. Remove the Front Fender from the bike

Remove the Front Fender from the bike once the remaining supports and fasteners are free.

Inspect the removed part and the mounting points before reassembly.

![Step 4 - remove the front fender from the bike](assets/screenshots/step_04_remove-the-front-fender-from-the-bike.jpg)

## Installation Procedure

### 5. Position the Front Fender for installation

Return the Front Fender to its mounting position and align the holes, tabs, or locating surfaces.

Hold the component in place until the first retaining hardware is started.

![Step 5 - position the front fender for installation](assets/screenshots/step_05_position-the-front-fender-for-installation.jpg)

### 6. Reconnect or refit the support pieces for the Front Fender

Reinstall any bracket, clip, spacer, linkage, or coupling associated with the Front Fender.

Make sure each supporting piece is seated correctly before final tightening.

![Step 6 - reconnect or refit the support pieces for the front fender](assets/screenshots/step_06_reconnect-or-refit-the-support-pieces-for-the-front-fender.jpg)

### 7. Install the retaining hardware for the Front Fender

Install the Front Fender retaining hardware by hand first so the part stays aligned in its mounting points.

Check that the component remains aligned while the hardware is brought fully home.

Tighten the front fender bolts to **8 Nm**.

![Step 7 - install the retaining hardware for the front fender](assets/screenshots/step_07_install-the-retaining-hardware-for-the-front-fender.jpg)

### 8. Verify the completed installation of the Front Fender

Inspect the final position of the Front Fender and confirm that all hardware, clips, and surrounding parts are back in place.

Check the component for correct fit and movement before returning the bike to service.

![Step 8 - verify the completed installation of the front fender](assets/screenshots/step_08_verify-the-completed-installation-of-the-front-fender.jpg)

## Critical Checks Before Riding

- Confirm the serviced component is fully seated and all fasteners are tightened in the correct order.
- Recheck all torque-critical hardware before riding.
- Make sure all routed cables, clips, brackets, and surrounding parts are returned to their original positions.
- Inspect the bike visually for any missing hardware before returning it to service.
