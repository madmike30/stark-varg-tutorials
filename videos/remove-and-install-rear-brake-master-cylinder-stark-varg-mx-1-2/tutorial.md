# Remove and Install Rear Brake Master Cylinder - Stark VARG MX 1.2 / EX

Source video: `Remove and Install Rear Brake Master Cylinder- Stark VARG MX 1.2` by Stark Future Official

Applicable models: `MX 1.2 / EX`

## Scope

This guide converts the official Stark Future tutorial video into a step-by-step workshop procedure. It documents each action shown in the video in sequence and pairs every step with a dedicated screenshot.

## Preparation

- Place the bike on a stable stand or work area before starting the procedure.
- Prepare the correct tools for the fasteners, clips, brackets, and components shown in the video.
- Keep removed hardware organized in sequence so reassembly follows the same order as the video.
- Have a torque wrench ready for any tightening steps that specify a torque value.

## Installation Procedure

### 10. Apply thread locker to both rear foot brake master cylinder to bracket bolts, then tighten them to 10 Nm

Apply thread locker to both rear foot brake master cylinder to bracket bolts, then tighten them to **10 Nm**.

![Step 10 - apply thread locker to both rear foot brake master cylinder to bracket bolts, then tighten them to 10 nm](assets/screenshots/step_10_apply-thread-locker-to-both-rear-foot-brake-master-cylinder-.jpg)

### 11. Carefully position the spring and spring housing, then insert the push rod into the rear foot brake master cylinder

Carefully position the spring and spring housing, then insert the push rod into the rear foot brake master cylinder.

![Step 11 - carefully position the spring and spring housing, then insert the push rod into the rear foot brake master cylinder](assets/screenshots/step_11_carefully-position-the-spring-and-spring-housing-then-insert.jpg)

### 12. Tighten the lever rod–to–brake pedal bolt to 10 Nm

Tighten the lever rod–to–brake pedal bolt to **10 Nm**.

![Step 12 - tighten the lever rod–to–brake pedal bolt to 10 nm](assets/screenshots/step_12_tighten-the-lever-rod-to-brake-pedal-bolt-to-10-nm.jpg)

### 13. Tighten the banjo bolt into the master cylinder to 27 Nm

Tighten the banjo bolt into the master cylinder to **27 Nm**.

Use new copper washers and install them on both sides of the brake hose. Ensure that the hose does not come into contact with the right side plate.

![Step 13 - tighten the banjo bolt into the master cylinder to 27 nm](assets/screenshots/step_13_tighten-the-banjo-bolt-into-the-master-cylinder-to-27-nm.jpg)

### 14. Refit the wrench and hose to the rear bleed screw so the rear brake system can be bled

Refit the wrench and hose to the rear bleed screw so the rear brake system can be bled.

## Critical Checks Before Riding

- Confirm the serviced component is fully seated and all fasteners are tightened in the correct order.
- Recheck all torque-critical hardware before riding.
- Make sure all routed cables, clips, brackets, and surrounding parts are returned to their original positions.
- Inspect the bike visually for any missing hardware before returning it to service.
