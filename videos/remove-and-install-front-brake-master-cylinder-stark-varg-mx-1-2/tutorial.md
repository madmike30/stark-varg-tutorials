# Remove and Install Front Brake Master Cylinder - Stark VARG MX 1.2 / EX

Source video: `Remove and Install Front Brake Master Cylinder - Stark VARG MX 1.2` by Stark Future Official

Applicable models: `MX 1.2 / EX`

## Scope

This guide converts the official Stark Future tutorial video into a step-by-step workshop procedure. It documents each action shown in the video in sequence and pairs every step with a dedicated screenshot.

## Preparation

- Place the bike on a stable stand or work area before starting the procedure.
- Prepare the correct tools for the fasteners, clips, brackets, and components shown in the video.
- Keep removed hardware organized in sequence so reassembly follows the same order as the video.
- Have a torque wrench ready for any tightening steps that specify a torque value.

## Installation Procedure

### 9. Place the master cylinder in its handlebar position

Place the master cylinder in its handlebar position.

Ensure the adjustment screw is free from contact with the throttle body.

![Step 9 - place the master cylinder in its handlebar position](assets/screenshots/step_09_place-the-master-cylinder-in-its-handlebar-position.jpg)

### 10. Tighten both bridge bolts to 6 Nm

Tighten both bridge bolts to **6 Nm**.

Ensure the arrow is pointing forward.

![Step 10 - tighten both bridge bolts to 6 nm](assets/screenshots/step_10_tighten-both-bridge-bolts-to-6-nm.jpg)

### 11. Tighten the banjo bolt into the master cylinder to 25 Nm

Tighten the banjo bolt into the master cylinder to **25 Nm**.

Use new copper washers and position them on either side of the brake hose.

![Step 11 - tighten the banjo bolt into the master cylinder to 25 nm](assets/screenshots/step_11_tighten-the-banjo-bolt-into-the-master-cylinder-to-25-nm.jpg)

### 12. Refit the wrench and hose to the bleed screw so the front brake system can be bled

Refit the wrench and hose to the bleed screw so the front brake system can be bled.

![Step 12 - fit the wrench onto the bleed screw to open and close it before attaching the hose to drain the brake fluid](assets/screenshots/step_12_fit-the-wrench-onto-the-bleed-screw-to-open-and-close-it-bef.jpg)

### 13. Bleed the braking system by adding brake fluid to the reservoir

Bleed the braking system by adding brake fluid to the reservoir.

![Step 13 - bleed the braking system by adding brake fluid to the reservoir](assets/screenshots/step_13_bleed-the-braking-system-by-adding-brake-fluid-to-the-reserv.jpg)

### 14. Pump the brake lever several times and, while holding it pulled in, open and close the front caliper bleed screw before releasing the lever

Pump the brake lever several times and, while holding it pulled in, open and close the front caliper bleed screw before releasing the lever.

![Step 14 - pump the brake lever several times and, while holding it pulled in, open and close the front caliper bleed screw before releasing the lever](assets/screenshots/step_14_pump-the-brake-lever-several-times-and-while-holding-it-pull.jpg)

### 15. Actively check the brake fluid level

Actively check the brake fluid level.

Ensure the level does not drop too low at any time.

![Step 15 - actively check the brake fluid level](assets/screenshots/step_15_actively-check-the-brake-fluid-level.jpg)

### 16. Carefully remove the hose from the front brake caliper bleed screw, then tighten the bleed screw and clean the area

Carefully remove the hose from the front brake caliper bleed screw, then tighten the bleed screw and clean the area.

Install the master cylinder reservoir cap and tighten both screws securely.

| Component | Torque |
| --- | ---: |
| Tighten both bridge bolts | 6 Nm |

Video-sourced torque items:

- Tighten the banjo bolt into the master cylinder: **25 Nm** from the official Stark tutorial video.

## Critical Checks Before Riding

- Confirm the serviced component is fully seated and all fasteners are tightened in the correct order.
- Recheck all torque-critical hardware before riding.
- Make sure all routed cables, clips, brackets, and surrounding parts are returned to their original positions.
- Inspect the bike visually for any missing hardware before returning it to service.
